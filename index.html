#The game notes

My grandfather taught me to keep score by hand. The card was the first clean dataset I ever held. Who was at the game, what happened, written down so it stayed true.

This is the same card, one dataset later. 178 games I was in the stands for, joined to the official record. Seven questions a fan asks, put through every tool you're being told will change everything. Each one made to show its work.

The tools: dbt on BigQuery holds the contract. Tableau, Tableau Next, Agentforce, Data 360, Snowflake, and Databricks are the doors. Claude Code kept the card with me. This site was made with the tools it describes.
